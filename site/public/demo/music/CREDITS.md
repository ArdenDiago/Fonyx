# Demo music

The songs the website demo can play. Every one was released by its artist under
CC0 (the Creative Commons public-domain dedication), so it may be copied,
changed and published without permission or credit. Credit is given anyway.

Each file was downloaded from Wikimedia Commons, whose page for it records the
CC0 dedication, then converted to MP3, levelled, and in one case shortened.
Before adding a song here, check its source page says CC0 or public domain.
