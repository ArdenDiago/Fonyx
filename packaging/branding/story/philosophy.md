# Signal Discipline

Time is the only material. Every composition in this movement is a strip of
time laid flat and measured: lanes run edge to edge like tracks on a
console, ticks fall at exact intervals, and the eye reads left to right the
way a clock does. Nothing floats. Each mark sits on a grid that was set out
before anything was drawn, and the grid stays visible, faint and patient,
so the viewer feels the order even where it is not obvious.

Colour is used the way a mixing desk uses light: a deep petrol field that
recedes, bone for anything steady and safe, amber for the brief moment of
change, signal red only where something broke. Red is rationed. It should
appear once, small and unmistakable, and the whole piece should be calibrated
around that one wound. The palette is never decorative; each hue has a single
job and keeps it everywhere it appears.

Scale does the talking. One statement is set enormous, in a tall condensed
face, stacked tight enough that the lines lock together like rack units.
Everything else is small, set in a monospaced voice with wide tracking, the
kind of label printed on a front panel. Between the two sizes there is
nothing: no medium type, no hedging. The contrast is meticulously judged so
that the monument and the annotation never compete.

Repetition carries the meaning. A row of identical bars says "this runs
forever, on time"; a single gap in that row says more than any sentence.
The work rewards a second look: the gap lines up exactly with the block
above it, the amber sliver is exactly as wide as it needs to be, the ticks
continue unbroken under both lanes. Those alignments are the product of
painstaking attention and are the real content.

Surfaces carry a fine grain, as if the piece were printed rather than
rendered, and the field glows faintly from within where the subject lives.
Margins are generous and symmetrical; nothing touches an edge, nothing
overlaps. The finished artifact should look like a page from the manual of
an instrument built by people at the top of their craft: master-level
execution, every measurement checked twice, quiet and exact.
