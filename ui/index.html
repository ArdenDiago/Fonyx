Compensation only ever adds delay to a faster output so it arrives closer to the slowest one in the group — it never speeds a slow device up. "Latency unknown" outputs (common over Bluetooth) are left at zero compensation rather than guessed.

Ducking

Cue ducks the rest on the cue output to %

When the source group's Level rises above threshold, every destination group ducks by Duck (dB), then releases smoothly over Release once the source quiets down.

Record a group

Recording taps the group's signal after its full DSP chain (EQ/compressor/gate/plugins) without touching its normal playback route. FLAC is lossless at 24 bits and about half the size of WAV.
